# DevQuest – International Online Hackathon (Tertiary Category of CODEFEST)

**DevQuest** is a premier international online hackathon under the **Tertiary category of CODEFEST**, created to spotlight the **talent, creativity, and innovation** of undergraduate and postgraduate students from universities and institutes across the globe.

---

## 🚀 What to Expect
- **1 intense round** of competition  
- **9 hours** of coding and problem-solving  
- **Fully online** – participate from anywhere  
- **Final Round**: August 27, 2025  
- A **2-hour online test run** will be held one week prior to help teams get familiar with the platform and format  

---

## 👥 Team Composition
- **1 to 3 undergraduate or postgraduate students**, all under the age of 36  
- All team members must be from the **same university or institute** and enrolled in the **same degree program** (e.g., BSc, MSc, MPhil, PhD)  
- Team members may belong to **different specializations** within the same program  
- Each team must include **one academic coach** from the same university or institute  

---

## 🏆 Awards & Recognition
In addition to **cash awards for the top 3 teams**, the **top 10 teams** and their **academic coaches** will receive certificates under the following categories:

- 🥇 **Winners (1st place)**  
- 🥈 **First Runner-up (2nd place)**  
- 🥉 **Second Runner-up (3rd place)**  
- 🎖️ **Merit Awards (4th–7th place)**  
- 📜 **Finalists (8th–10th place)**  

---

## 🛠️ Required Skills
Participants should be proficient in:
- **Git** – collaborative version control  
- **JavaScript, HTML, CSS** – core web development technologies  
- **SQL** – managing and querying relational databases  

---

## 📜 Rules & Regulations
1. Only **registered team members** are eligible to compete.  
2. **Team composition cannot be changed** after registration. No replacements will be allowed.  
3. A participant may be part of **only one team**.  
4. Teams will be **ranked based on total points** earned by solving problems.  
5. In case of a tie, the **earlier final commit** will be ranked higher.  
6. The **judges’ decision is final** and binding.  
7. To maintain fairness, individuals who are **current employees, interns, or former interns of 99x Technology** are respectfully requested **not to participate**.  

---

## 📅 Key Dates
- **Test Run**: One week prior to the final round (2 hours)  
- **Final Round**: August 27, 2025
